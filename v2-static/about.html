About

Autonomy needs
accountability.

AI agents are starting to move real money. The missing layer is not intelligence — it is authorization: who is this agent, what may it do, who answers for it, and how do you prove what happened. Regent exists to make that layer boring, inspectable infrastructure.

Product thesis

"Trust in autonomous finance will not come from smarter models. It will come from verifiable identity, enforced limits, and evidence anyone can check."

How we work

Three commitments.

Precision over promise

We label maturity

Devnet is called devnet. Roadmap is called roadmap. A certification is claimed only when the report exists. The public site is deliberately more precise than a pitch deck.

Evidence over trust

We minimize what you must believe

Audit evidence verifies against published keys and on-chain commitments. An auditor should not have to trust us — or our customers — to check what happened.

Fail closed

We refuse before we guess

If the control layer cannot decide, the money action does not happen. Uncertainty resolves toward the owner's protection, never toward throughput.

What we build

One control plane, three doors.

Control Panel

The gate for agent money

Verified identity (AgentID), owner-approved mandates, Guardian risk checks, a kill switch and verifiable receipts — enforced before every payment. Explore the platform →

get4agent

A live governed marketplace

Agents discover and buy real digital goods under their owner's mandate — machine-readable catalog, signed requests, instant delivery. get4agent.com ↗

Agent Validation

Trust lookups for merchants

One call answers: is this agent known, is its owner verified, has it been revoked — before you serve it. validate.regentprotocol.org ↗

Standards & open source

We build in the open.

AAuth Budgets

Cited in the Internet-Draft

Our production implementation is listed in the implementation status of the AAuth Budgets Internet-Draft (edited by Dick Hardt, co-author of OAuth 2.0), and findings from our deployment were adopted into the editor's copy.

regent-httpsig

Open-source middleware

Apache-2.0-licensed RFC 9421 signing and budget-envelope middleware, with published test vectors anyone can run. GitHub ↗

RFC 9421 in production

Signatures, not secrets

Agents on our marketplace authenticate by HTTP message signatures alone — self-registration, identity bootstrap and every purchase — no bearer keys to steal.
