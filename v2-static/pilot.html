The engagement

What the 14 days contain.

Days 1–3

Architecture session: your workflow, where the mandate gate sits, what the evidence pack must contain for your reviewers.

Days 4–10

Integration: AgentID issuance, mandate design for your limits and approvals, Guardian rules tuned to your risk signals, audit chain configured.

Days 11–14

Validation: a governed action end to end — allowed, denied, held — with the evidence trail your compliance team can inspect. Dedicated support channel throughout.

Pilot FAQ

Before you write to us.

How long is the pilot engagement?
14 days of structured work with our engineers, then a decision: continue on a paid plan, extend the evaluation, or part ways with your integration notes in hand.
What do we need to prepare?
A workflow where an agent takes financial actions, one engineer with API access, and the limits you want enforced. We bring the rest.
Is there a free way to evaluate first?
Yes — the Developer tier is free and self-serve: register agents, write mandates, authorize actions on devnet before talking to anyone.
Can we switch plans later?
Yes. Plans meter verified actions, so moving between tiers is a billing change, not a re-integration.
What happens after we apply?
We reply by email with a short qualification call proposal — typically within two business days.
