A business with its own Stripe account

Support · billing · marketplaces

Your own Stripe connects after you go live: an identity check and a passkey, about five minutes. From then on every refund, charge, credit, coupon and payment link your agent attempts is decided against your limits first, and the Stripe key stays in Regent's vault. The agent never holds it.

  1. Sign up and confirm your email.
  2. Verify your identity (a document and a selfie, about three minutes), then press Go live on the same screen. It is one-way.
  3. Add a passkey in Settings: budgets and approvals are signed with it.
  4. In Stripe: Developers → API keys → Create restricted key. Write: Payment Intents, Charges, Refunds, Transfers, Invoices, Webhook Endpoints. Read: Account information. Add Payment Links, Coupons or Credit notes only if your agent uses them: the key stays the outer wall.
  5. In the console: Connect → Gateway → paste the rk_… key. Regent registers the webhooks itself.
  6. Connect the agent with one of the boxes below, or point a Stripe agent at Regent instead of mcp.stripe.com:
claude mcp add --transport http stripe https://gw.regentprotocol.org/gw/mcp/stripe

Tested by us in Stripe test mode: payment, payment link, refund, transfer to a seller and invoice, each closed by Stripe's own webhook. Before you go live the same tools run on Regent's Stripe test account. Platforms that pay sellers: see the Stripe page for the extra permissions. Stripe App: soon in the Stripe Marketplace.

The beta, plainly

Free for three months
Sandbox for 30 days, then go live, free for three months in total from the day you sign up. Regent never asks for a card to use Regent itself. We will tell you the price at least 30 days before your three months end.
Test money first
A new account is a sandbox: Stripe in test mode, test USDC, and at most $100 per payment. It lasts 30 days; going live keeps everything you built in it.
Real money needs a verified owner
You verify your identity once. Then you can connect your own Stripe account or your own card. Card purchases through Agentcard need a US card and an owner aged 18 or over.
We never hold your money
Payments run on your Stripe account, your card or your wallet. Regent decides; the payment provider moves the money.
What we see
Amounts, merchants, your limits and every decision. Never your card number. Identity documents stay with the verification provider.
It is a beta
Offered by Regent Protocol Inc., a Delaware corporation. Things will change and some will break. We fix fast and write down what changed. See the terms and the privacy policy.
