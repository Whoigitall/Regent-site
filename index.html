The Challenge of Autonomous AI Agents

Anonymity Risks

AI agents operate without clear identity, creating AML compliance challenges and legal accountability gaps in financial transactions.

Legal Vacuum

Unclear liability frameworks and emerging regulations like the EU AI Act require new infrastructure for agent accountability.

Black-Box Decisions

Opaque AI decision-making processes undermine trust and prevent institutional adoption in regulated finance.

17.5%

AI agents projected to handle 17.5% of digital commerce by 2035 (Tietoevry)

Our Solution: KYA Infrastructure

Know Your Agent — The first comprehensive identity and oversight platform for autonomous AI agents in finance

AgentID

Digital passport system combining decentralized identifiers (DID) with verified owner KYC. Each agent receives a unique, immutable identity linked to a responsible legal entity.

  • ▸ W3C DID-compliant credentials
  • ▸ Owner KYC verification
  • ▸ Cross-border compatibility

Audit Chain

Immutable blockchain-based audit trails capturing every agent decision, data source, and action. Full transparency for regulators and stakeholders.

  • ▸ Timestamped decision logs
  • ▸ Tamper-proof record storage
  • ▸ Regulatory-ready reporting

Guardian AI

Real-time behavioral monitoring and anomaly detection with explainable AI (XAI). Proactive guardrails prevent regulatory breaches before they occur.

  • ▸ Continuous compliance monitoring
  • ▸ XAI-powered explanations
  • ▸ Automated risk alerts
